Starting...

Large downloads take a while. If a firewall asks, click Allow. Do not close this window: closing it stops setup and removes what was installed so far.

Next steps:

  1. In the control panel that opens, add your own voice: a clean recording of one speaker. 3 to 10 seconds works best; up to 20 seconds is accepted. The sample voices you ticked are already there.
  2. In the VoiceStudio+SimpleVoice OBS Connector extension (0.2.0 or newer) open Settings, section 2, choose SimpleVoice OBS (or click Detect) and click Load voices in section 3.
  3. Keep your OBS Browser Source URL as it is.
