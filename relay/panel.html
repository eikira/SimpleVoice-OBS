Add a voice








WAV, FLAC, MP3, AAC, M4A or OGG: a clean recording of one speaker. 3 to 10 seconds works best; up to 20 seconds is accepted and longer recordings are refused. No music. Cloning needs the graphics card engine. The words are written down automatically for you to check.







Check the words

Listen, and fix the text so it matches exactly what is said. Better text gives a better voice.











Test









Voice: who speaks. Speed: how fast, 1.0 is normal speaking speed. 







Made as WAV: mono, 16-bit, 24 kHz. Saved files are always mono and 24 kHz; MP3 and AAC use 128 kbps.
